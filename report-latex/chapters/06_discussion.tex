\section{Project Contributions}
The proposed system demonstrates that computer vision and queueing theory can be combined to produce actionable real-time indicators.
Uncertainty quantification is a major contribution because it helps avoid decisions based on overconfident estimates.

\section{Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item simplifying assumptions of the M/M/1 model,
    \item sensitivity to detection quality in complex scenes,
    \item dependence on proper zone configuration,
    \item current lack of native multi-zone support in the analytics core.
\end{itemize}

\section{Comparison with Field Requirements}
The prototype meets real-time monitoring and decision-support needs.
For large-scale operational deployment, an additional industrialization and extensive evaluation phase is still required.
